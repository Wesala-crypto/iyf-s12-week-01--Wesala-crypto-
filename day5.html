My Journey Into Web Development

Published on October 6, 2026 by Christopher

Getting Started

Learning web development has introduced me to the world of HTML, CSS, and programming. My journey started with understanding how websites are structured.

HTML has helped me learn how to create headings, paragraphs, links, images, forms, and other important parts of a webpage.

What I Have Learned

One of the most important lessons I have learned is that good websites should be accessible, organized, and easy for users to navigate.

I have also learned how Git and GitHub can be used to track changes and store my projects online.

Looking Ahead

I plan to continue improving my coding skills by building more projects and learning new technologies.

Every project gives me an opportunity to practice what I have learned and become a better developer.
